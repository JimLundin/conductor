Relationships

FromToCardinalityMeaning

Derived, never stored twice

  • A session's Work, Issue and Project follow from its Worktree.
  • A Work's Sessions are the sessions in its Worktree.
  • A Worktree's lineage follows from its Work's continues; continuing always makes a Work.

Policy (changes without changing the model)

  • A worktree runs one turn at a time: a second session's turn there waits for the running one to end.
  • A worktree is cleaned when its Work finishes, or after seven idle days. Uncommitted changes are checkpointed and the branch pushed or kept before the directory goes.
  • The home never records where a host keeps a checkout; a host finds its checkouts by root commit and remote.
