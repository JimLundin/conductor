Keep the depth that earns its place

What I would retain in this round

  • Two provider adapters, managed CLI installs and private homes. Their seam is real: two native protocols and recorded fixtures justify the implementation. Removing installation mostly transfers setup and verification to the owner.
  • Durable sessions, reconnect/replay, approvals, questions and commit snapshots. These support supervision and recovery directly; internalizing their types alone would not remove their obligations.
  • Web Push and sub-agent visibility. Both add code, but background attention and child requests support phone supervision. No usage evidence justifies sacrificing them ahead of the cuts above.
